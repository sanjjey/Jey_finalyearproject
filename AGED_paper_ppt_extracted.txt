OUTLINE
IDENTIFY ASPECT–GLOBAL EVALUATIVE DISSONANCE
 EXAMINE HOW CONSUMERS’ EVALUATIONS OF SPECIFIC PRODUCT ASPECTS CAN DIFFER
FROM THEIR OVERALL PRODUCT EVALUATION.
EXAMINE THE ROLE OF PRIOR REVIEW ENVIRONMENTS
 ANALYSE WHETHER THE OPINIONS, RATINGS, AND SENTIMENT IN EARLIER REVIEWS
INFLUENCE HOW CONSUMERS EVALUATE PRODUCT ASPECTS IN SUBSEQUENT REVIEWS.
ANALYSE THE TEMPORAL RELATIONSHIP
 INVESTIGATE HOW THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN ASPECT-LEVEL EXPERIENCES AND
GLOBAL EVALUATIONS CHANGES OVER TIME AS THE REVIEW ENVIRONMENT EVOLVES.
DEVELOP AND VALIDATE THE AGED FRAMEWORK
 QUANTIFY PATTERNS OF ASPECT–GLOBAL DISSONANCE AND DETERMINE WHETHER
PRIOR REVIEW ENVIRONMENTS SYSTEMATICALLY EXPLAIN VARIATIONS IN THIS
DISSONANCE, CONTRIBUTING TO A MORE DYNAMIC UNDERSTANDING OF ONLINE
CONSUMER EVALUATION.

  SCOPE & PROBLEM STATEMENT
EXISTING RESEARCH STUDIES ASPECT-LEVEL EVALUATIONS, GLOBAL EVALUATIONS, AND PRIOR REVIEW INFLUENCE
LARGELY IN ISOLATION.
IT REMAINS UNCLEAR HOW THE PRIOR REVIEW ENVIRONMENT SHAPES THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN A CONSUMER’S
CURRENT ASPECT-LEVEL EXPERIENCE AND OVERALL PRODUCT EVALUATION.
THE ROLE OF ASPECT-LEVEL MISMATCH/DISSONANCE BETWEEN PRIOR REVIEWS AND CURRENT EXPERIENCE IN
SHAPING GLOBAL EVALUATION REMAINS UNDEREXPLORED.


--- PAGE 6 ---
RESEARCH CHALLENGES
 ACCURATELY IDENTIFYING WHEN ASPECT-LEVEL OPINIONS CONFLICT WITH THE OVERALL
PRODUCT EVALUATION.
 IDENTIFYING PRODUCT ASPECTS AND THEIR ASSOCIATED SENTIMENT FROM UNSTRUCTURED,
DIVERSE REVIEW TEXT.
 DEFINING AND MEASURING WHAT A CONSUMER WAS EXPOSED TO IN EARLIER REVIEWS
WITHOUT CONFUSING PRIOR INFLUENCE WITH GENERAL PRODUCT TRENDS.
 SEPARATING GENUINE CHANGES IN CONSUMER EVALUATION OVER TIME FROM CHANGES
CAUSED BY PRODUCT UPDATES, REVIEW COMPOSITION, OR OTHER EXTERNAL FACTORS.


--- PAGE 7 ---
RESEARCH OBJECTIVES
TO EXAMINE HOW CONSUMERS’ EVALUATIONS OF
INDIVIDUAL PRODUCT ASPECTS INFLUENCE THEIR
OVERALL/GLOBAL EVALUATION OF THE PRODUCT.
TO INVESTIGATE HOW THE PRIOR ONLINE REVIEW
ENVIRONMENT SHAPES CONSUMERS’ INTERPRETATION OF
THEIR CURRENT ASPECT-LEVEL EXPERIENCES.
TO EXAMINE WHETHER AND HOW THE PRIOR REVIEW
ENVIRONMENT MODERATES THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN
CURRENT ASPECT-LEVEL EVALUATIONS AND CONSUMERS’
GLOBAL PRODUCT EVALUATIONS, PARTICULARLY WHEN
THERE IS A MISMATCH BETWEEN PRIOR REVIEWS AND
CURRENT EXPERIENCE.

Identifies AGED as a distinct form of divergence between aspect-level and

product experiences.
Captures temporal changes in consumer evaluations as the review
environment evolves.
Provides a dynamic framework for understanding online consumer decision-
