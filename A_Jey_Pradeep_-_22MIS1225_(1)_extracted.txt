Integrates previously separate streams of online-review research by showing how the
prior review environment, current aspect-level evaluation, and global product evaluation
operate together rather than as independent processes.
Reframes the role of prior reviews from simply influencing consumer opinions to
potentially changing how consumers translate their own product experiences into overall
judgments.
Explains why similar aspect-level experiences can produce different overall evaluations by
introducing the mismatch between the prior review environment and the current
experience as a mechanism shaping global evaluation.
